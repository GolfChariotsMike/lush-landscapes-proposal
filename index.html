The flagship feature

Photo to
fixed-price quote.
No site visit.

No other landscaping quoting tool does this. A homeowner takes a few photos of their backyard and receives a real, accurate, fixed-price quote in their inbox — without anyone leaving the office.

The A4 paper acts as a known reference object (210×297mm) in the photo. AI detects it, establishes scale, measures the area, and generates a schematic site plan the customer approves before the quote is issued. The approved plan is stored in the CRM — protecting Lush from scope disputes.
