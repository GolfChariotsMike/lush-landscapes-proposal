The flagship feature

Photo to
fixed-price quote.
No site visit.

A homeowner takes a few photos, places an A4 sheet on the ground, and receives a real fixed-price quote in their inbox — without anyone leaving the office.

1
Customer takes 3–4 photos

Overhead + ground-level angles. Place a blank A4 sheet flat on the ground in at least one shot.

2
A4 paper establishes scale

AI detects the 210×297mm reference, calculates real-world dimensions from every angle.

3
AI generates a 2D site plan

Schematic overhead diagram with boundaries, dimensions, and features labelled. Customer approves it before any money is discussed.

4
AI produces an accuracy score

Every quote shows a confidence rating. High confidence = fixed-price quote issued immediately. Low confidence = site visit recommended automatically.

5
Fixed-price quote delivered

Measurements feed into your rate card. Branded quote sent by email and SMS for customer approval.
